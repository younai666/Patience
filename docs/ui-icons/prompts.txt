[sessions]
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original tiny editorial UI artwork for an Android plain-text writing app, one square asset only, displayed at 32dp.
Primary request: Illustrate a small collection of THREE overlapping irregular ivory note sheets; the front sheet has TWO very thick near-black short hand-drawn horizontal strokes. A clear metaphor for opening a directory of writing sessions.
Input images: all three are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY, not edit targets. Transfer their simple irregular carriers and thick rounded ink behavior; do not reproduce their literal objects.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat full-bleed opaque oat #E3DACC covering all four corners; no transparency and no white border.
Subject: a small collection of THREE overlapping irregular ivory note sheets; the front sheet has TWO very thick near-black short hand-drawn horizontal strokes. A clear metaphor for opening a directory of writing sessions. One focal cluster centered and occupying 75–80% of the square so it remains legible when reduced to 32dp.
Style/medium: Anthropic editorial illustration language; naive ink gesture with thick slightly uneven rounded strokes, visible analog wobble, simplified flat forms and deliberate asymmetry. Large bold silhouette, extremely sparse detail.
Color palette: irregular ivory #FAF9F5 carrier shape; uniform near-black #141413; one oat #E3DACC background field.
Composition/framing: 1:1 square, close composition, generous but not excessive outer margin. The irregular ivory carrier must materially separate the symbol from the background.
Materials/textures: absolutely uniform flat colors, NO lighting variation, NO grain, NO shaded paper, no gradients.
Text: none.
Constraints: preserve the two-layer system of full-frame accent behind irregular ivory carrier with bold hand-drawn marks on top; entirely opaque. This is an ORIGINAL artwork.
Avoid: extra objects, decorative stars, hands, shadow, perspective, transparent canvas, black canvas, white outer canvas, 3D, glossy shading, photorealism, fine technical line art, geometric stock-vector polish, dense detail, logo lettering, watermark, copied reference composition.
